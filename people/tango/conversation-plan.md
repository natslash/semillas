## Before the meeting

## Conversation outline
1. **Open with the purpose (first minute).** "I want to talk about serious concerns about how the PE role is working. This is a difficult conversation and I'd rather be direct."
2. **Name the relationship once.** "We know each other from before. That's exactly why I'm being straight with you. It doesn't change how I assess this."
3. **Feedback: themes plus specific examples.** Low availability; vague answers when teams ask for help; teams now work around him; california's tasks not delivered to the expected quality; written feedback acknowledged but nothing changed. Facts and impact, not labels. No names for team-level feedback.
4. **His understanding.** "How do you see this? Had you been aware of it?" Then listen. Note what he accepts, disputes, or explains.
5. **Be explicit about the process.** 2 weeks pre-PIP → formal 6-week PIP, with written expectations and regular check-ins. Don't soften this.
6. **His task for the 2 weeks.** Come back with his own view: the gaps he recognises and what he will deliver. This becomes input to the written PIP expectations.
7. **Support.** Weekly check-ins with me; access to california and the teams to agree deliverables; anything that's blocking him.
8. **Close.** Summarise and confirm the next meeting date.

## After
