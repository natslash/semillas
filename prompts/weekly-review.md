# Weekly review (Fridays, ~30 min)

Input: Shashi's raw notes for the week, pasted in. May contain real names — replace per AGENTS.md §1.

1. Split the notes by stream (person or meeting). For each, write one entry in the correct ledger using the template. Facts and my read strictly separated; hunches marked "(speculation)".
2. Extract every commitment (`who → what → by when`). List the ones that fall due next week at the top of your reply.
3. Update `70-threads.md`: for each thread touched this week, add a dated line linking to the entry. Open a new thread only if something recurred three times untagged.
4. Update `10-stakeholders.md` and `80-skip-levels.md` in place where a read changed or a new IC was met.
5. Check `90-encounters.md`: anyone appearing for the second time gets promoted to `10-stakeholders.md`.
6. Flag, in this order, max 5 bullets: (a) contradictions between what different people said, (b) commitments that are stale or at risk, (c) anything not heard about a team in >3 weeks, (d) a change in Shashi's own read since last week.
7. Draft the Friday update per `prompts/friday-update.md` if asked.
8. Propose a commit message.

Do not editorialise beyond step 6. Do not add advice unless asked.
