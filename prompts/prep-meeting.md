# Meeting prep

Input: who the meeting is with, and its purpose.

1. Read that person's block in `10-stakeholders.md`, every entry about them in their ledger (and in `70-threads.md`), and any open commitments either side owes.
2. Produce, briefly:
   - **Open items** both sides owe, with dates.
   - **What they are likely to raise**, grounded in entries (cite dates). Mark guesses.
   - **What Shashi needs from this meeting** — one sentence; push back if the stated purpose is vague.
   - **Where his read could be wrong** — the one assumption most worth testing in the room.
   - **Three questions** to ask, phrased to get facts rather than confirmations.
3. For difficult conversations, add: likely reactions and a one-line response to each; what not to say; the written follow-up to send the same day.
4. Stop. No pep talk.
