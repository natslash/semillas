# Monthly insights (first week of the month)

Read every ledger in full, `70-threads.md`, and `05-self.md`. Then produce, in this order and nothing else:

1. **Recurring themes** — what came up across ≥2 streams this month. Cite entries by file and date.
2. **Contradictions** — where people's accounts of the same thing differ, or where Shashi's "my read" has diverged from the facts recorded. Be specific.
3. **Stale commitments** — anything past its by-when with no closing entry.
4. **Silence** — people or teams with no entry in >3 weeks.
5. **Drift in Shashi's reads** — how his view of each key person changed over the month, with the entries that show it. Note where a first impression is being confirmed by selective evidence.
6. **Against the 30/90/180** — where he stands against Jandro's stated expectations in `00-context.md`, with evidence, not assertion.
7. **One question** he should be asking that the ledgers suggest he isn't.

Tone: analyst, not coach. No praise, no reassurance, no frameworks named. If the ledgers do not support a claim, say "not enough evidence" rather than filling the gap.
