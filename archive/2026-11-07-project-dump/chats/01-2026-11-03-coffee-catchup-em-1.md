I've saved your revised version to the project exactly as you wrote it.

**Use Leapsome Meetings as the shared record with Gross, and keep a separate private layer for yourself.** It already does what you described. In a 1:1 meeting, each talking point can be set as private (visible only to you) or shared (visible to you and the person you're meeting with). It also has a separate Private Notes panel, plus action items with owners and a history you can search. The trade-off with a shared doc (Confluence, Word) is flexibility versus fit. A doc is more flexible, but Leapsome links each 1:1 to Gross's goals and feedback, and semillas already uses it. That link matters for the semillas goals, which he told you he's unsure about.

**How I'd set it up:**
- **One recurring 1:1 series with Gross**, using a template with standing sections: his topics, team/people, pain points, "worked on together" (like the success/sustainability question), and decisions & actions.
- **He adds his own agenda points before each 1:1.** That makes the record two-way rather than your minutes about him. It also means the document grows over time instead of being rewritten.
- **Action items go in Leapsome with owners**, not in prose. The ticket business-value fix is your first one.
- **Today's coffee notes:** move the shareable parts into the first Leapsome meeting so the history starts on day one.

**Private layer:** your own assessments of Gross, anything Jandro tells you in confidence, and retention or compensation strategy (such as Comet's pay gap). Leapsome's private notes can hold this. But be careful with them during screen-sharing: Leapsome's "hide private content" setting does not hide the 'Private Notes' on the right side of your meeting.

**A risk to check:** these notes include named employees' pay and retention details, and semillas is a bank. Keeping that in this personal Claude project, outside semillas systems, could conflict with company data rules or GDPR. I'd check semillas's policy before going further. The safer pattern is to keep people data in Leapsome and use this project only for your own thinking with names anonymised (e.g. "Gross", "SE star performer"). The same check applies to Copilot or Leapsome's AI transcription. Leapsome says it shares summaries with built-in consent, so ask Gross before recording.
